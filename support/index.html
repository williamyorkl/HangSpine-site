Where is my data stored?

手表没有自动计数？
请在手表上开始训练后握紧单杠保持静止 1–2 秒，App 通过加速度计识别悬垂姿态； 短暂换手（4 秒内）不会中断计数。

No auto-counting?
Keep still on the bar for 1–2 seconds after starting a session; quick regrips (≤4s) won't reset the current rep.
