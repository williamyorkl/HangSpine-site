FAQ

How do I start a session?

Open HangSpine on your Apple Watch and tap Start. After a 3-2-1 countdown, grab the bar — the watch detects your hang automatically. No taps needed mid-workout. By default the set ends when you reach your per-hang goal and a rest countdown starts — see “Can I change the per-hang goal or my daily goal?” below for the other option.

The watch isn't counting. What should I check?

Keep still on the bar for 1–2 seconds after mounting — the watch recognizes the hanging posture via its motion sensors. When you hang freely, a quick regrip (under 4 seconds) stays part of the same hang; in a hangboard protocol, every drop ends the rep. On the free plan, automatic detection pauses once your 100 auto-detected sessions are used up — manual timing keeps working, and Pro removes the limit. If detection feels off, see the adaptive detection question below.

Can I change the per-hang goal or my daily goal?

Yes. For an unplanned free hang, set the target per hang from 5 to 180 seconds: turn the Digital Crown on the watch's Ready screen (any second in that range), or in the iPhone app open Dashboard, tap the gear icon and choose Free hang goal → Seconds per hang (preset steps from 10 to 180 s). A plan carries its own hang time, which you edit in the plan editor and which takes precedence over this default. The daily free-hang target is Free hang minutes in the same Settings screen. What happens at that goal is set by Goal behavior on the same screen. End at goal and rest is on by default: the set ends at the goal and a rest countdown starts (30–180 s, your choice). Turn it off and each hang is timed to its full length until you let go. Changes sync between the watch and the phone.

What is adaptive detection (Feedback Learning)?

Everyone's body and bar setup are different. The experimental Feedback Learning feature records the motion trace of each session and periodically tunes detection to your personal mount/dismount pattern — entirely on your device. It is on by default, and you can turn it off. In the iPhone app, open Dashboard, tap the gear icon and look under Feedback Learning: the switch is Feedback Learning (Experimental), and View detection traces is where you review and label your traces.

Where is my data stored?

Your sessions live on your iPhone and Apple Watch and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database (an automatic mirror). No account, no tracking, no ads. One exception: when you ask the AI coach for a plan, the sentence you type and your training profile go to the plan service (a Cloudflare Worker that forwards them to DeepSeek). Web addresses, email addresses and mainland Chinese mobile numbers are removed on your device before anything is sent — from the current message and from earlier messages sent again as context. The plan service runs its own removal on the current message again before forwarding or logging it. A redacted copy of the current message is kept for up to 90 days to improve the coach. See the Privacy Policy.

Does it require HealthKit permission?

HealthKit access is optional — it only saves completed workouts to Apple Health. Declining it does not affect counting or statistics. You can change this anytime in the Watch app: Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → HangSpine.

How do I switch the app language?

In the iPhone app, open Dashboard, tap the gear icon → Language. The choice applies to both the iPhone and Apple Watch apps immediately.

How do I erase my data?

iPhone app → Dashboard tab → gear icon → Data section. Erase all sessions removes every session from this iPhone and from your private iCloud copy; it cannot be undone. Erase all data also clears your training profile, plans and settings, and also reaches your other devices: the paired Apple Watch is reset, and other iPhones on the same iCloud account drop the erased sessions the next time they sync.

Free and Pro

What does the free plan include?

HangSpine is free to download. Free includes your first 100 auto-detected sessions — a lifetime total, not a monthly one — your full history, all stats, iCloud sync and 5 AI coach questions a day. After 100 auto-detected sessions, automatic detection pauses and the watch offers a manual timer instead. Nothing is deleted.

What does HangSpine Pro add?

Pro removes the auto-detection limit and raises the AI coach allowance from 5 to 50 questions a day on the monthly plan, or 100 a day with the one-time purchase. You choose between a monthly subscription and a one-time purchase. In the US, Pro is US$3.99 a month or US$19.99 once; other regions: see the App Store.

I already bought HangSpine. Do I have to pay again?

No. You're a founding member: lifetime Pro, with nothing to do. The app recognizes you automatically. An install that already has sessions is recognized at once; a reinstall or a new phone is recognized through your original App Store purchase on the same Apple Account (this can need a network connection and may complete on a later launch).

How do I restore Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling?

In the iPhone app, open Dashboard, tap the gear icon → HangSpine Pro, then tap Restore Purchases. It uses the App Store purchase history of the Apple Account you are signed in with. Your sessions come back on their own if you were signed in to iCloud before.

How do I manage or cancel the monthly subscription?

The monthly plan renews automatically until you cancel. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose HangSpine Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. In the app, the HangSpine Pro screen also has a Manage subscription button. Buying the one-time Pro does not cancel a running monthly subscription — cancel it yourself, or it will keep renewing.

Does Pro support Family Sharing?

The one-time Pro purchase can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing. The monthly subscription cannot be shared.

How many questions can I ask the AI coach per day?

Free: 5 a day. Pro monthly: 50 a day. Pro one-time (and founding members): 100 a day. The count resets at midnight on your device. A request that fails because of a network or service problem is not counted, and answers from the on-device coach never count.
