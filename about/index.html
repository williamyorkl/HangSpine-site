ABOUT

Hi — I'm William, and I hang from a bar most days

HangSpine is a one-person studio. I build an Apple Watch app that counts dead hangs, and free tools and guides like the ones you're browsing — because good information about spines is rarer than it should be.

Why this exists

A few years ago, hanging became part of my own daily spine-care routine — a minute on the bar, shoulders opened, spine unloaded after long days at the desk. It's the cheapest “gym” there is. What I lacked was a way to keep the habit honest: something that counted the hangs for me instead of asking me to tap a timer with numb fingers. So I built HangSpine: mount the bar and the count starts, let go and it stops.

Along the way I read a lot of scoliosis research — partly out of personal interest, partly because hanging kept getting recommended to people with curved spines with wildly different confidence levels. The guides on this site are my attempt to organize that reading honestly: cited, hedged where the science is thin, and free of miracle language.

What we make

  • HangSpine for Apple Watch — an automatic dead-hang counter and streak tracker. One-time purchase, no subscription, no account.
  • Free web tools — starting with the dead hang time calculator. No signup, no ads, no cookies.
  • Evidence-first guides — primers and reviews with their sources attached.

Editorial standards for health content

  1. Claims carry citations. Health statements link to the study or guideline behind them. When the evidence is thin, we say so in the same sentence.
  2. No treatment language. We don't claim anything here treats, cures or corrects scoliosis. Hanging is framed as an adjunct habit; the evidence-based backbone of scoliosis care is clinician-delivered.
  3. Commercial disclosure. When a page relates to something we sell (the app), the connection is stated on the page. The guides are written to be fair whether you buy anything or not.
  4. Clinical review status. These guides are written by the developer, not a clinician. We are seeking independent review for the scoliosis, osteoporosis and stenosis pieces; until a reviewer signs off, they remain developer-written and cite primary sources.
  5. Corrections welcome. If a source is misrepresented or a study has been superseded, tell us and we'll fix it.

Contact & transparency

Questions, corrections, or just want to compare hang times: reach out via the support page. On Reddit, the studio operates one official account (u/HangSpineTeam) — every post from it is posted as the developer, never disguised.
